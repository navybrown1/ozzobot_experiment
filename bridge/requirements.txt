# OzoPet bridge dependencies.
#
# The released ozobot-evo 0.3.2 on PyPI cannot drive a real Evo natively:
# its native driver raises NotImplementedError and its ozobot-ble
# dependency is not published on PyPI. The native (bleak/CoreBluetooth)
# driver is available in the official Ozobot monorepo, so the Ozobot
# packages are installed from a pinned commit by bridge/install.sh.
#
# Run:  ./install.sh
#
# Third-party dependencies of the Ozobot packages (installed by install.sh,
# listed here for reference/CI):
loguru
bleak~=2.0.0
pydantic>=2.11.5
ozobot-web>=0.3.0,<0.4.0
