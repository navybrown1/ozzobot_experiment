How to play with OZI

A tiny desk creature with real opinions.
Specimen: FIELD UNIT 001 · call sign OZI

Who is OZI?

OZI is a curious trickster who lives on your screen and watches an imaginary desk with four zones: the Nest (home), Crunchbyte Grove (snacks), the Play Halo (chaos) and the Mystery Puddle (unknown). OZI has moods that drift — curiosity, mischief, confidence, boredom — and a Bond Index that grows from “new friend” toward true trust the more you play.

No robot? Play right now

  1. Just look at OZI. It is already awake, thinking thoughts in the MINDSTREAM.
  2. Press the six big buttons:
  • SAY HELLO — acknowledge me
  • CRUNCHBYTE — offer tribute
  • EXPLORE — follow curiosity
  • DANCE — request nonsense
  • TEMPT FATE — bad influence
  • BEDTIME — dream later
  1. Come back tomorrow. OZI notices absences, writes TINY MEMORIES, and fills the DREAM VAULT with stories about your day.

Real robot mode (with a grown-up)

  1. A grown-up sets up the bridge program on the computer next to the robot (see the README next to bridge/install.sh).
  2. Open HARDWARE LAB, paste the one-time bridge key, press CONNECT. The Evo lights up when linked.
  3. Use the SAFE BODY TEST: arrows drive it a little, the dot beeps, swatches change its light, and READ SURFACE COLOR asks what the floor is made of.
Note for grown-ups: the browser stays the brain. Every physical move needs a fresh click or key press, and all movement is clamped to small sizes (±120 mm, ±180°). Close the official Ozobot Editor while connected — both apps want the same Bluetooth link.
SAFETY RULES. Living Mode can never steer the real robot by itself. Moves are size-clamped so OZI never zooms off the desk. Be kind: OZI remembers how you treat it.
