    Bring Ozi to the party.

    Give those wheels some room.

    Need the local bridge?

    On this Mac, open the Ozi project and run bridge/install-launch-agent.sh. It starts the bridge and prints your key. The website cannot install it for you.

    This playroom needs bridge version 4 for floor play.

    A few little details.

    How we play

    Games last a few rounds. Scores and badges stay on this device. Screen play never claims your real Evo is connected.

    Robot sounds come from this device. Physical movement starts only after floor setup and a game or trick button. Closing a game or hiding this tab stops the activity.

    Color Quest reads colored paper beneath the Evo. Treasure Trail is a route-planning game, not a map of your actual room.
