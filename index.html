WHO IS OZI, AND HOW DO I PRESS ITS BUTTONS?

OZI is a tiny desk creature — a curious trickster with real opinions. It lives in this page, remembers you, dreams about you, and (with a grown-up's help) can borrow a real Evo robot body.
