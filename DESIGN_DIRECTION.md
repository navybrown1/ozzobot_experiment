# OzoPet visual direction

The interface is intentionally styled as a strange late-1990s field manual for a living electronic specimen rather than a generic AI dashboard.

Principles:
- warm paper, black ink, cobalt, safety orange, acid green, yellow, and translucent toy colors
- thick printed borders, registration marks, receipts, punch cards, labels, and physical control surfaces
- asymmetrical editorial layout instead of glass cards
- the habitat is a tabletop field map, not a dashboard chart
- Ozi should look like a transparent toy/electronic creature with visible circuitry
- playful copy remains, but the visual system should feel designed by a human studio rather than generated from a SaaS template
